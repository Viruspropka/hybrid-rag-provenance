# Platform team charter

## What the team owns

The platform team owns the deploy pipeline, the job queue, backups, the on-call rotation and the shared
Postgres and Redis clusters. Product teams own their services and alerts but use the platform runbooks.

## How to reach the team

Ask in the platform help channel during working hours; page the platform on-call engineer for production
issues outside them.
